    How buying works

    1. 1

      Pick a canvas

      Open any work to see it up close. Some pieces also have a photo on a real wall, which helps you judge the size.

    2. 2

      Send one message

      Tap “Buy this piece” and the message is written for you, with the title, size and price. You only have to send it.

    3. 3

      Make it yours

      Zephy confirms the piece is yours and arranges payment and pickup or delivery with you. Nothing is paid on this site.
